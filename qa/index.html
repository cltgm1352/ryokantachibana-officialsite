Q&A

01 Are pets allowed?
We are sorry, but pets are not permitted at our inn.
02 Can I stay alone?
We primarily welcome families with children and are generally unable to accommodate solo guests.
03 Is it family-friendly?
Absolutely. TACHIBANA warmly welcomes families with children. We have prepared an environment where you and your little ones can relax and feel at home.
04 What is the maximum number of guests?
Up to 4 guests. If your party includes infants, please contact us in advance.
05 Is there parking available?
Yes, free parking is available on site.
06 Do you offer a shuttle service?
Yes, transfers to and from the nearest Urakawa Station can be arranged upon request. Please let us know at the time of booking.
07 Can I use the kitchen?
Yes. A mini kitchen equipped with a rice cooker, microwave, and basic cookware is available for your use.
08 Are meals provided?
Meals are not provided. You are welcome to use the kitchen, or we can recommend nearby restaurants.
09 Is Wi-Fi available?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.
10 What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 3:00 PM. Check-out is by 11:00 AM.
11 How do I make a reservation?
Reservations are accepted exclusively through Airbnb. Please book via the link below.
Book Now →
12 What is the cancellation policy?
Our cancellation policy follows Airbnb's terms. Please refer to your booking page for full details.
13 Are there tourist attractions nearby?
Urakawa offers walks along the Tenryu River, tea field visits, and firefly watching. Seasonal experiences can also be arranged.
14 Can I experience local culture?
On request, we can introduce you to local tea-making workshops and traditional preserved food experiences.
15 Is there a place for children to play?
The inn is surrounded by nature. Children can enjoy playing in the river, catching insects, and exploring the outdoors.
16 How would you describe TACHIBANA in one sentence?
"Time spent together, hearts set free." A quiet mountain village where Japan's original landscape meets the warmth of family.
